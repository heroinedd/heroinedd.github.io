About Me

My research focuses on network verification and automated reasoning. I develop symbolic and distributed simulation methods for network verification (SRE, Expresso, and S2), as well as techniques for efficient analysis of access control policies in public clouds (ReLIA).

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Xi’an Jiaotong University, in the NetVerify Lab within the ANTS group, under the supervision of Prof. Peng Zhang. I also visited the SysLab at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Ratul Mahajan.

Research Interests

  • Networking: network verification through symbolic and/or distributed simulation.
  • Automated reasoning: access control policy analysis on public clouds.
